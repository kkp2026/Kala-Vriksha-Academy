About Us

Kala Vriksha Academy is a vibrant platform dedicated to preserving, promoting, and celebrating the rich traditions of Indian fine arts in Canada.

Rooted in India’s cultural heritage and growing within Canada’s multicultural landscape, the Academy brings together vocal music, instrumental music, and dance under one artistic umbrella.

Our programs are guided by qualified, experienced, and recognized teachers and accomplished artists who bring strong training, performance experience, and a deep understanding of traditional art forms.

Objectives

  1. To promote and preserve Indian fine arts through education, performance, and community engagement.

  2. Nurturing excellence in vocal, instrumental, and dance traditions.

  3. To foster cultural connection, artistic excellence, and appreciation of India’s rich heritage within Canada’s multicultural community.
